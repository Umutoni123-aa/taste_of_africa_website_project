About Us

Our Mission

Taste of Africa exists to celebrate and protect African food heritage. We share recipes, stories, and traditions so that people everywhere can enjoy the flavors of our continent.

A Short History of African Cuisine

African cuisine is thousands of years old. It grew from local crops like teff, yam, and cassava, and from trade routes that brought new spices and ideas.

Today, African dishes like Jollof Rice and Tagine are loved around the world. African flavors have also shaped the food of the Caribbean, the Americas, and beyond.
