Culinary Regions

West Africa

West African food is bold and full of flavor. Meals are often built around rice, yams, and stews made with peppers and palm oil.

Staples

  • Rice
  • Yam
  • Plantain

Cooking techniques

Slow simmering, frying, and one-pot cooking.

Example dishes

  • Jollof Rice

East Africa

East African food uses grains, leafy greens, and fermented foods like teff and cassava leaves. Meals are often shared.

Staples

  • Teff
  • Cassava
  • Beans

Cooking techniques

Fermenting, pounding, and boiling.

Example dishes

  • Injera
  • Isombe

North Africa

North African food uses warm spices like cumin and saffron, with slow-cooked stews and couscous.

Staples

  • Couscous
  • Lamb
  • Olives

Cooking techniques

Slow cooking in a clay pot, and roasting.

Example dishes

  • Tagine
